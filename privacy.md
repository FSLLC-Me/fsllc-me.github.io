# Free Solutions Inventory Sync — Privacy Policy

Free Solutions LLC operates this private application to connect its own eBay seller account for inventory management.

## Information stored
The application stores seller account identifiers and encrypted access and refresh tokens in Cloudflare D1. This version does not import buyer details or orders or change listings.

## How information is used
Information is used to maintain the authorized connection, not for advertising or resale. eBay and Cloudflare process information needed to provide the service.

## Retention and deletion
Verified eBay account-deletion notifications remove matching account records from the active database. Provider backups may remain until their retention period expires.

You can revoke access through your eBay account. Revoking access alone does not delete stored records. Contact us to request removal of retained connection records.
